# Alfanar Projects — presenter notes

20-minute run of show. Suggested durations include reading, transitions and pauses.

## 01. Engineering trust.  Creating commercial opportunity. 

90 seconds

Open with the business question: how can marketing help the right stakeholders understand, trust and engage Alfanar Projects? My recommendation starts with the company’s existing position as a global project developer, engineering construction and technology solutions provider with roots in Saudi Arabia. It preserves the corporate tagline, For the world. With the world. The opportunity is to make that promise more tangible through documented project evidence and clearer buyer journeys.

Over the next twenty minutes, I will connect brand leadership with commercial priorities and show exactly where AI can help execution. Grid and Renewable Energy are my proposed starting priorities, subject to leadership and business-development validation. They do not replace the wider portfolio. This is a candidate assessment, with recommendations clearly distinguished from official company facts. I will close with decisions for the first ninety days and a six-month measurement plan. Pause briefly to establish that the objective is qualified commercial influence, with a strong and consistent master brand.

## 02. A strong evidence base.  A partial performance picture. 

90 seconds

The evidence supports a strong foundation of capability and project communications. It does not establish marketing effectiveness. That distinction matters because a large visible audience can still contain relatively few of the people involved in a procurement decision. The supplied assessment identifies corporate news, project milestones, events and employer content, with an opportunity to deepen technical and buyer-specific narratives. I treat that as an outside-in interpretation, not a completed analytics audit.

I would use the first thirty days to resolve the unknowns. Marketing and business development should agree the account universe, map channel ownership and connect website analytics with inquiry and CRM definitions. We also need a consistent sample of recent content. The output should be a baseline that leadership can use to make investment decisions. No follower growth, conversion rate or revenue uplift appears here as a verified result. The thirty-day figure is a proposed management deadline. Ask the panel to consider whether the organisation currently has a shared definition of a qualified marketing inquiry.

## 03. Grid and Renewables lead.  The whole portfolio benefits. 

90 seconds

I propose Grid and Renewable Energy as initial campaign priorities because the available project evidence gives us concrete stories for both. This is a recommendation based on the assessment, not a claim about Alfanar’s approved commercial plan or revenue mix. Before allocating spend, business-development leaders should validate where the pipeline, delivery capacity and strategic account opportunities justify attention.

For Grid, the audience includes utilities, grid planners and energy developers. The content should help them understand relevant engineering experience and the role of storage and grid infrastructure. For Renewables, we should distinguish project developers from industrial energy buyers, because their questions and buying processes differ. The rooftop solar project gives us a tangible industrial example without implying that every opportunity has the same scope or economics.

The master-brand system still serves Water, Infrastructure, Process and Digital. Jazan provides an Infrastructure proof point within the flagship campaign. I would organise content by market-facing business line while routing ownership and approvals through the correct operating business. This avoids confusing the website’s commercial navigation with the legal group structure. No new market entry or geographic ranking is assumed.

## 04. Different stakeholders need  different reasons to engage. 

100 seconds

A single project announcement can serve several audiences, but it should not ask all of them to take the same action. Buyers need confidence that the company has relevant experience for the opportunity they are considering. Consultants and engineers need enough technical clarity to understand applicability. Institutional stakeholders may care about sector development and documented contributions. Partners need clarity about complementary capabilities and responsibilities.

I would create a priority-account list jointly with business development and map buying roles within it. That list should include the account owner, sector, market, current relationship, relevant evidence and intended next action. The marketing team can then decide whether a technical briefing, an executive perspective, an event conversation or a project case study best supports the relationship.

This is where brand activity becomes commercially useful without reducing every communication to a lead form. A relevant consultant reading and reusing an approved technical case study may be a meaningful outcome even before an inquiry occurs. The account owner should validate that interpretation. Marketing should record engagement consistently and avoid claiming that a single content interaction caused a later contract award. Leave a short pause for the audience to read the four stakeholder rows.

## 05. Five pillars.  One marketing operating system. 

100 seconds

The five pillars connect strategy with day-to-day execution. Audience and brand intelligence sets priorities. Technical storytelling turns approved experience into useful evidence. Digital discovery makes that evidence easier to find and understand. Commercial and institutional engagement helps teams use it in relevant relationships. AI operations and measurement supports all four rather than operating as a separate content factory.

For example, an account insight can identify a recurring question about storage integration. Engineering can approve the factual answer and project scope. Marketing can turn that into a project page, a technical briefing and a LinkedIn narrative. Business development can use the same approved material in an account conversation. Measurement then tells us which audience engaged and whether the interaction progressed.

As Section Manager, I would own the common brief, editorial standards and review cadence. Sector specialists own technical accuracy. Business development owns account qualification and follow-up. A weekly production review resolves blocked assets, a monthly review connects activity to account engagement, and a quarterly review adjusts investment. AI helps summarise evidence, prepare variants and reduce repetitive production work. It cannot authorise technical claims or determine that an account is commercially qualified. The value is a more dependable process across the portfolio.

## 06. Discovery starts with  clear, attributable answers. 

100 seconds

Search and AI discovery should begin with clarity of evidence. SEO helps people find the relevant capability page. Answer-engine optimisation helps that page directly address a buyer’s question. Generative-engine optimisation aims to make the organisation’s facts and sources easier for AI systems to identify and cite. These are related editorial and technical practices, not guaranteed ranking products.

The first step is a technical audit of crawlability, indexation, metadata, internal links and the routes from sector pages to project evidence. I have not reviewed the unavailable SEO/AIO/GEO PDF, so I am not presenting a technical score, a ranking deficit or a crawler failure as a verified finding. The work shown here is a proposed audit and implementation plan.

For the example question, a useful answer would name Bisha, explain Alfanar’s contribution, identify the client and link to the official source. We should also provide a clear way to discuss relevant capabilities. Any structured data should accurately describe the visible page. To measure progress, use Search Console and analytics for queries and qualified visits, plus a fixed, dated set of AI-answer prompts. Record the model and answer sources because outputs can vary. Discuss changes as observations rather than proof of causal uplift.

## 07. Social content should earn  relevant attention. 

100 seconds

The social assessment is qualified by the evidence available. The supplied report describes LinkedIn activity across corporate updates, projects, events and people. It also contains differing follower snapshots. I have therefore omitted the follower headline and would establish one dated baseline with the channel owner. The ownership and performance of the other channels need confirmation, especially where Alfanar Group accounts might be confused with Projects accounts.

My proposal is to sample thirty recent LinkedIn posts and twenty videos where a verified channel exists. Categorise topic, format, intended audience, technical depth, call to action and publicly observable response. Median public interactions can describe that sample, but they do not substitute for a reach-based engagement rate. For audience quality we need geography, seniority, industry and account relevance from analytics.

The content rhythm should combine project proof with buyer education and the corporate narrative. One approved case study can support several formats without repeating identical copy. Arabic and English versions need editorial and technical review. Before increasing publishing volume or paid spend, establish which content brings relevant people to useful pages and which interactions business development considers meaningful. This gives us a basis for channel decisions beyond follower count.

130 seconds

The after text is the AI-assisted draft recorded in the report. I am not presenting it as a newly executed model experiment or an approved company publication.

We could evaluate factual error rate, reviewer time and approved output quality in a controlled pilot. No such improvement has been measured here. The demonstration shows a change in writing, not a proven business result.

## 10. Measurement follows  the buying journey. 

100 seconds

The scorecard follows the buying journey. Relevant awareness asks whether we are reaching people who matter to the chosen accounts and sectors. Evidence engagement asks whether those people use the material. Commercial progression asks whether business development accepts the inquiry or meeting as relevant. Operating quality checks that the team can produce accurate material reliably.

Before reporting a percentage, we need to agree its denominator, event definition, source and owner. For example, an inquiry acceptance rate is accepted inquiries divided by all recorded inquiries in the same reporting period, with duplicate and spam handling defined. An engaged visit requires a specific agreed event, not an arbitrary label applied after the campaign.

I would set numerical targets after the baseline review at day thirty, using account size, historical performance, available budget and sales capacity. Invented growth promises would weaken the plan. We should also separate opportunities that marketing sourced from those where marketing influenced an existing relationship. That distinction prevents double counting and does not claim causation. Every monthly review should end with a decision about targeting, content, the website journey or follow-up. For long procurement cycles, six months may show qualified engagement and opportunity progression before it shows attributable revenue.

## 11. Ninety days to establish proof.  Six months to improve the system. 

100 seconds

The first ninety days should produce a working system and an investment decision. Month one establishes account priorities, the evidence library, channel ownership and measurement access. That month also resolves technical and client approvals so the team can produce material from a stable brief.

The five-week campaign pilot sits within months two and three. Its first week confirms permissions and approvals, its second produces the assets, and its third launches the Bisha story. The fourth introduces Jazan and rooftop solar, with the fifth reserved for optimisation and review. These are relative campaign weeks rather than a promise tied to calendar dates. If a project’s permissions are incomplete, its publication waits while approved work continues.

By day ninety, I want an approved source library, a functioning campaign hub, reliable inquiry routing and a review of relevant account response. The review should determine whether to continue the same audience, revise the story or pause spend. Months four through six improve the best-supported activity and extend the content system to other business lines where commercial priorities justify it. The five-week activation and six-month programme therefore have distinct purposes: the activation tests the content and distribution, while the longer period tests the operating rhythm and commercial contribution. Take a short pause for the panel to read the decision gates.

90 seconds

The resourcing recommendation uses a small coordinating team and named business reviewers. The Section Manager owns priorities, quality and the performance conversation. Shared content and design specialists prepare the assets. Digital and web support implements the journeys and analytics. Business development owns account qualification and engineering owns technical review. These are proposed responsibilities, not a claim about the current headcount.

I would cost the pilot once the asset scope, footage availability and web requirements are clear. Budget categories include production, web implementation, approved AI tooling and a capped paid distribution test. Leadership can release spend in stages, using the baseline and pilot evidence to guide the next commitment.

The main risks are manageable if we give them owners. Project claims need source and scope approval. Logos, photographs and client references need appropriate permissions. Slow reviews need named reviewers and agreed turnaround. Data gaps need an access plan. AI use needs approved tools and clear rules for confidential information. Long sales cycles require leading indicators rather than an unsupported revenue promise.

My request is approval for a ninety-day pilot, validated account priorities and access to the relevant analytics and CRM information. The intended result is clearer capability understanding and more useful commercial conversations, supported by a repeatable system. Close here and invite questions about priority accounts, review capacity or the first campaign story.

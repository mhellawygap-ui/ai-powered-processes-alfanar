# Alfanar Projects — presenter notes

Cover + 12 assessment slides + closing. An 18-minute plan within a 20-minute slot.

Full audit: https://drive.google.com/file/d/13HXkiHICC3RhMyAwfMhG80sDRb2v37LX/view?usp=sharing

## Cover. Strengthening Alfanar Projects’  brand awareness 

30 seconds

I started with Alfanar’s existing story: its projects, its people and its international engineering experience. My plan is to make that impact more visible to the audiences that matter to the business. I’ll show the research, the early fixes and a practical six-month programme.

Purpose: Marketing Section Manager · Second-round assessment

## 01. Alfanar already has  a strong story 

65 seconds

I spent time understanding Alfanar before deciding what to recommend. The company already has substantial experience and an established corporate position. I would strengthen how that story reaches the right people and helps them understand the company’s relevance. These three areas connect brand management with business development and everyday execution.

Purpose: Executive vision & brand awareness

## 02. What I found when  I looked at Alfanar 

65 seconds

My review combines the public company story with a technical and search audit conducted on 15–16 October. The audit measures website issues directly, uses Semrush to estimate search demand, and tests how AI systems describe Alfanar. Those sources identify useful opportunities, while Alfanar’s own analytics and BD feedback would show which audiences and interactions matter most commercially. The existing project and corporate presence gives us a strong foundation to build on.

Purpose: Current brand presence & research findings

## 03. We’re visible. Are we reaching  the right people? 

85 seconds

The top one hundred traffic-driving search terms are mainly company names, jobs and project names. None are buyer searches for the services Alfanar sells, such as an EPC contractor or a substation contractor. The targeted EPC prompt did recognise Alfanar, but ChatGPT linked to the Group website, which shows that recognition does not always direct buyers to Projects. My recommendation is to make the service pages and company identity clearer so more potential customers can discover the right capabilities.

Purpose: Commercial search & AI visibility

## 04. Some opportunities  are quick to fix 

105 seconds

These are practical improvements that marketing and IT can work on together in the first month. Clear page descriptions improve how links appear, crawl and language signals help search engines understand the site, and lighter media gives mobile visitors a better experience. For context, LCP measures when the main visible content appears, and the audit reports five point three seconds against a two point five second good-experience threshold. I would also build a useful Arabic electrical-contracting page around the demand identified in the audit, supported by relevant project examples.

Purpose: Website, SEO, AEO & GEO opportunities

## 05. Who do we need to reach  and why? 

75 seconds

I would separate potential customers from the people who influence their decisions and from institutions that strengthen our position in the industry. Utilities and project owners need to see why Alfanar is relevant to an opportunity, consultants need confidence in the technical expertise, and institutions need a clear reason to collaborate. The named organisations illustrate the types of relationships I would prioritise with BD. Saudi Arabia would be the starting market, with Grid and Renewables leading the initial campaign while the whole portfolio remains visible.

Purpose: Target audiences, entities & objectives

## 06. Our work. Our people.  Our impact. 

80 seconds

One point I raised in the interview is that the human side of Alfanar deserves more visibility. The official company story already talks about employee development, local skills and supplier participation. I would bring those subjects into project stories and share the outcomes of CSR programmes in terms people can understand, such as the skills developed or the community benefit delivered. That gives audiences a fuller picture of the company alongside its engineering achievements.

Purpose: Brand positioning, people & social impact

## 07. Four things  I would focus on 

75 seconds

I would execute four connected priorities rather than start a long list of separate initiatives. Each has an owner, a clear benefit and a way to review progress. AI and measurement support the whole system, including the twelve automation ideas in the research, beginning with a small project-to-content pilot. Weekly production and monthly marketing/BD reviews keep the team focused on useful outcomes.

Purpose: Integrated marketing priorities

## 08. One story. Different channels.  Different audiences. 

75 seconds

My social review found a strong existing flow of corporate and project updates on LinkedIn. I would build on it with more engineer perspectives, technical explanations and human stories. The website provides the full evidence, while LinkedIn, video, industry media and events adapt it for the people using each channel. This lets a single story support both public communication and a relevant BD conversation.

Purpose: Content, social, video, PR & events

## 09. How AI would help us  work better 

105 seconds

I used Bisha to demonstrate how AI can turn accurate project facts into a clearer explanation for utility leaders. The revision introduces the grid challenge and explains what storage can help with, while keeping the client, capacity and Alfanar’s contribution accurate. The same approach helps with buyer questions, social posts, scripts, reporting and event preparation. Engineering and marketing still own the facts and publication decision.

Purpose: AI integration & practical demonstration

## 10. Engineering the Future,  Project by Project 

90 seconds

Each project gives a different audience a reason to consider Alfanar. Bisha demonstrates storage and grid capabilities, Jazan shows EPC delivery for an infrastructure destination, and rooftop solar brings the energy transition into an industrial setting. I would add an engineer or team perspective to each story so the campaign also shows the people behind the work. The five-week pilot introduces the stories, with subsequent months improving distribution and supporting relevant conversations.

Purpose: Flagship integrated campaign

## 11. How will we know  it’s working? 

85 seconds

I want the monthly report to explain what changed for the audience and the business. We can show whether the website works better, more people find the services, the right stakeholders understand the company, and more relevant conversations reach BD. The CRM connects campaign contacts with meetings and opportunities, while an awareness check shows whether recognition improves. I would calculate financial return where the connection is clear and also show opportunities where marketing supported an existing relationship.

Purpose: KPIs, awareness measurement & ROI

## 12. My first 90 days  and the following six months 

105 seconds

The first month validates priorities and starts the website quick wins. The five-week campaign pilot runs within Months Two and Three, with rights and technical approval completed before each story launches. By Day Ninety, I want approved evidence, connected reporting and a clear decision on the next investment. Shared specialists and named reviewers make the plan practical, while staged spending, confidentiality controls and monthly reviews manage the main risks.

Purpose: Six-month roadmap, resources & risks

## Closing. For the world. With the world.™

40 seconds

Alfanar already has a strong story to tell. My plan connects its engineering achievements with the people and impact behind them, reaches more relevant audiences and helps marketing demonstrate its contribution. I would start with a ninety-day pilot, agreed priorities and named owners. Thank you, and I welcome your questions.

Purpose: Discussion & next steps
